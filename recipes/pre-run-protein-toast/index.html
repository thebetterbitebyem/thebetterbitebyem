Pre-Run Protein Toast

The ultimate pre-run snack: cottage cheese toast. Sourdough and dates fuel your run, cottage cheese keeps you full, and it works just as well after your run. Shout out to Finks Bakery (@finks.london) for the sourdough.

Ingredients

Method

Prefer video? Watch me make it

  1. Slice the sourdough.

  2. Smash the dates onto the toast.

  3. Spoon over the cottage cheese.

  4. Air fry for 2 minutes at 180°C.

  5. Drizzle with honey and enjoy.

Em's tip

More time? Soak the dates in boiling water for 3–5 minutes for a jammy texture.
