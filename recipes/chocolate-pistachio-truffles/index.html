Chocolate Pistachio Truffles

The no-bake treat that tastes like a truffle! A fudgy chocolate centre that you'd never guess is made from dates and tahini, dipped in a white chocolate and pistachio coating. 6 ingredients, no oven.

Calories
210 kcal
Time
No bake, 30 min in the freezer
Makes
20 truffles

Ingredients

Chocolate base

Pistachio coating

Method

Prefer video? Watch me make it

  1. Soak the dates in boiling water, then drain.

  2. Blend the dates and tahini, then add the cocoa and salt.

  3. Roll into 20 balls and freeze for 30 minutes.

  4. Melt the white chocolate and stir in the pistachio cream.

  5. Dip each ball, let it drip, then sprinkle with chopped pistachios.

Em's tip

Use medjool dates for extra sweetness!
