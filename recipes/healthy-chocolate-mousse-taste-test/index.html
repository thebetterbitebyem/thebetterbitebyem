I Tried 6 Healthy Chocolate Mousse Recipes

I tried 6 healthy chocolate mousse recipes so you don't have to. Three high-protein bases (cottage cheese, Greek yogurt and silken tofu), each made two ways: with dates and cocoa, or with melted dark chocolate. Then I ranked them on taste, texture and protein.

How I tested them

Same cocoa, same maple, same night in the fridge. The only things that changed were the base and the sweetener.

  1. Add the sweetener to each base: dates and cocoa, or melted dark chocolate and cocoa.
  2. Blend until silky smooth.
  3. Label and cover.
  4. Chill overnight, then taste them side by side.
