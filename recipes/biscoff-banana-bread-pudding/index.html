Biscoff Banana Bread Pudding

Peak is… upgrading your daily yogurt bowl to this. Layers of Biscoff biscuit, vanilla protein pudding, banana and melted Biscoff, with 20g of protein in one jar.

Watch the reel on Instagram on Instagram (opens in a new tab)
Protein
20g
Time
5 min
Makes
1 jar

Per jar: 20g protein. These are estimates and vary with the brands you use.

Ingredients

Method

Prefer video? Watch me make it

  1. Crush the biscuits. Press some into the bottom of a jar for the base and save the rest for the top.

  2. Spoon in a layer of the protein pudding.

  3. Add a layer of banana slices.

  4. Drizzle over some of the melted Biscoff.

  5. Repeat the layers until everything is used up.

  6. Finish with the rest of the biscuit crumble on top.

Em's tip

Look at those layers! Build it in a glass jar so you can see them.
