Viral Apple Salad

The viral apple salad with a magic date caramel. Crunchy green apple, Greek yogurt, almond butter and chocolate chips in a jar: lid on, shake, stir and dig in.

Method

Prefer video? Watch me make it

  1. Blend the dates and tahini into a caramel.

  2. Chop the apple into a jar.

  3. Add the yogurt, almond butter and caramel.

  4. Top with the chocolate chips and cinnamon.

  5. Lid on, shake, stir and dig in.

Em's tip

Make a big jar for the whole week!
