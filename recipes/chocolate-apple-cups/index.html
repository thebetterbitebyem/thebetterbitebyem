Chocolate Apple Cups

Apple season is here! Melted dark chocolate cups filled with a soft date, crunchy almond butter and chopped apple, then covered in more chocolate. The "caramel" is just a date.

Watch the reel on Instagram on Instagram (opens in a new tab)
Calories
280 kcal
Time
No bake, 30 min in the fridge
Makes
6 cups

Per cup: about 280 kcal. These are estimates and vary with the brands you use.

Ingredients

Method

Prefer video? Watch me make it

  1. Spoon chocolate into 6 muffin cases.

  2. Add a date and a dollop of almond butter to each.

  3. Pile on the apple, then cover with chocolate.

  4. Sprinkle with seeds and chill for 30 minutes in the fridge.

  5. Peel off the cases and enjoy.

Em's tip

Keep them in the fridge!
