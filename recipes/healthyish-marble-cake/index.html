Healthyish Marble Cake

Cake for breakfast? This healthyish marble cake is naturally sweetened with apple sauce and honey and made with almond flour. 7 ingredients and one loaf tin, with an optional hazelnut butter and chocolate orange topping.

Method

  1. Whisk the apple sauce, eggs and honey.

  2. Mix in the almond flour, baking soda and vanilla.

  3. Halve the batter and stir the cocoa into one half.

  4. Layer both into a lined loaf tin and swirl.

  5. Cover with foil and bake for 50 minutes at 170°C.

  6. Let it cool, then add the topping if you like.

Em's tip

It's not too sweet as it is, so add more honey to taste.
