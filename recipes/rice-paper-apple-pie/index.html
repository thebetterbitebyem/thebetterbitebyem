Rice Paper Apple Pie

The easiest, lightest apple pie you'll ever eat. Jammy cinnamon apple wrapped in rice paper, brushed with maple syrup and air fried until it's crunchy outside and soft inside.

Calories
160 kcal
Time
Air fry 12 min at 200°C
Makes
2 pies

Per pie: about 160 kcal. Excludes the yogurt. Add 100g Greek yogurt for +10g protein. These are estimates and vary with the brands you use.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Cook the apple with maple syrup, cinnamon and cornflour until sticky.

  2. Soften the rice paper and wrap up the filling.

  3. Brush with maple syrup.

  4. Air fry for 12 minutes at 200°C.

  5. Drizzle with maple syrup and top with walnuts.

  6. Serve with Greek yogurt.

Em's tip

Vanilla protein yogurt works too!
