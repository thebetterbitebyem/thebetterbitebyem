Oreo Cheesecake Ninja Creami

Creamy and dreamy, just like Oreo cheesecake. The base is 5 simple ingredients with no protein powder and no artificial sweeteners, just banana and maple.

Ingredients

Method

Prefer video? Watch me make it

  1. Blend it all, then top up with almond milk to the max fill line.

  2. Freeze overnight in the Creami tub.

  3. Spin on the lite ice cream setting.

  4. Add the crushed Oreos and spin on the mix-in setting.

Em's tip

No Creami? Blend everything and freeze it in a tub, then add the Oreos and re-blend before serving.
