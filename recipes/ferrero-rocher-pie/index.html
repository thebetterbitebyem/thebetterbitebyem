Healthyish Ferrero Rocher Pie

A fudgy date and hazelnut base, a silky chocolate mousse layer and a crunchy hazelnut top. The secret to that mousse? Silken tofu. No bake, no cream, just 7 ingredients.

Method

Prefer video? Watch me make it

  1. Blitz the dates, hazelnuts and almond flour, then add the cocoa and blitz again.

  2. Press the mixture into your tin.

  3. Blend the tofu, the ⅔ cup of melted chocolate and ½ jar of hazelnut butter until silky.

  4. Spread over the base and smooth it over.

  5. Mix the rest of the melted chocolate with ¼ jar of hazelnut butter and spread it on top.

  6. Sprinkle with chopped hazelnuts and chill until set.

Em's tip

You'd never guess the mousse is made with silken tofu. Blend it until it's completely smooth for the silkiest filling.
