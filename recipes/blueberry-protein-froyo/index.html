Blueberry Protein Froyo

POV: froyo's trending again and you're not paying £10 a tub. Greek yoghurt swirled with frozen blueberries under a white chocolate shell you crack into. Creamy, crunchy, and no ice cream maker needed.

Protein
18g
Calories
230 kcal
Time
5 min + 10 min in the freezer
Makes
1 pot

Per pot: 18g protein, 20g carbs and about 230 kcal. These are estimates and vary with the brands you use.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Sweeten the yoghurt, if you like.

  2. Swirl in the frozen blueberries.

  3. Melt the buttons and stir in the almonds.

  4. Spread the topping over the yoghurt and freeze for 10+ minutes.

  5. Crack it, mix it up and enjoy!

Em's tip

The Greek yoghurt gives you 15g of protein to keep you full, and the white chocolate is the treat bit: just 15g. The numbers leave out the optional sweetener.
