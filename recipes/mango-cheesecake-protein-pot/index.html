Mango Cheesecake Protein Pot

The 13g protein snack that tastes like mango cheesecake! Three ingredients, ready in 2 minutes.

Protein
13g
Calories
200 kcal
Time
2 min
Makes
1 pot

Per pot: 13g protein and about 200 kcal. These are estimates and vary with the brands you use.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Start with half a pot (120g) of the cottage cheese.

  2. Add the frozen mango chunks.

  3. Mix it all together.

  4. Sprinkle the seeds on top and enjoy!

Em's tip

The cottage cheese brings the protein and live cultures, the frozen mango adds vitamin C and fibre, and the linseed and hemp in the seed mix add omega-3s and crunch.
