Apple Crumble Breakfast Oats

Apple crumble… for breakfast?! Warm cinnamon apple, a crunchy almond and oat crumble and vanilla protein yogurt, layered in a jar. Like dessert for breakfast.

Protein
21g
Calories
320 kcal
Time
Cook, toast, then layer
Makes
1 jar

Per jar: 21g protein and about 320 kcal. Estimate with GetPro vanilla protein yogurt. These are estimates and vary with the brands you use.

Ingredients

To top

Method

  1. Cook the apple with cinnamon until soft.

  2. Toast the oats and almonds, then add the maple syrup.

  3. Stir in the almond flour and yogurt to make clumps.

  4. Layer the apple, crumble and protein yogurt.

  5. Dust with cinnamon, lid on and enjoy.

Em's tip

Make double the crumble for tomorrow!
